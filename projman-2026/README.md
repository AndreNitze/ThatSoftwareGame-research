# ProjMAN 2026: methods and selected evidence

Version **1.0.3** accompanies the formative design case **Beyond the Game: Designing a Facilitated Project Management Learning Environment — A Multi-Year Case Study** by **André Nitze**, Brandenburg University of Applied Sciences.

This folder provides the coding frame, selected anonymous evidence, and aggregate metadata for third-party scrutiny of the paper. It is separate from the software research snapshot cited at the repository root. No supplement DOI or paper DOI is claimed.

## Contents

- [Methods supplement](methods-supplement.md): source roles, full inclusion/exclusion criteria, segmentation, multiple coding, and design interpretation.
- [Standalone codebook](codebook.md): all twelve stable code names, English definitions, boundaries, and examples.
- [Selected evidence](selected-evidence.csv): **24** anonymous segments covering the manuscript’s cited examples and the full codebook; stable source/segment IDs and original wording are retained.
- [Source index](source-index.csv): source types, collection periods, and recorded cohort versions where event assignments are documented; one workbook source does not represent one participant.
- [Code reach](code-reach.csv): separate primary, contextual-summary, and Product Owner counts for all twelve codes.
- [Collection contexts](collection-contexts.csv): twelve documented questionnaire blocks plus one unassigned record, version labels, and aggregate denominators.
- [Corpus summary](corpus-summary.json): primary/contextual/PO separation and the closed-item orientation result.
- [Design chronology](design-chronology.md): dated implementation sequence with evaluation/exposure boundaries.
- [Citation metadata](CITATION.cff) and [checksums](SHA256SUMS).

## Corrected evidence base

There are **168** anonymous questionnaire records, not necessarily 168 distinct people. The three actual open fields contain **286** substantive cells from **138** records. Together with **17** group mind maps, they yield **719** primary segments (**713** coded, **1,021** assignments). Two contextual summaries (**15** segments) and eight Product Owner summaries (**18** segments) are separate sources and do not increase student frequencies. These source roles account for the original 752 archival segment IDs without deleting or renumbering them.

Mind maps are prompted group products. Counts are pooled across heterogeneous contexts/builds and do not establish population prevalence, current defect prevalence, learning gains, or transfer. A colleague with no prior involvement in game development or the study reviewed the codebook and code assignments, judged them valid, and raised no objections. No checked-segment count, independent-recoding procedure, or intercoder-reliability coefficient is reported.

## Institutional assessment

After historical data collection, the Chair of the Senate of Brandenburg University of Applied Sciences reviewed the study as a whole, including its methodology, questionnaires, and target group, and assessed it as ethically unobjectionable on **14 August 2026**. This was an institutional assessment after collection, not a claim of prospective ethics-committee approval.

## Availability and reuse

The selected evidence is provided for checking the reported examples, not as the complete response corpus. Original workbooks, mind-map files, participant identities, personal filenames, exact response timestamps, instructor notes/identities, and application source code are not included.
